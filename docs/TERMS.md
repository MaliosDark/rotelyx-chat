# Terms of use

Last updated: 12 September 2026. Rotelyx is published by Ideoa Labs, and the
address for anything in this document is <contact@ideoa.co.uk>.

There is no account to agree to this with. Using the application is the
agreement, and stopping using it is the end of it.

## What you may not do with it

Rotelyx carries messages between people who have paired with each other. You
may not use it to send, ask for, or store:

  * Anything sexual involving a child. There is no context in which this is
    tolerated and it is reported to the authorities of the jurisdiction we can
    reach.
  * Threats of violence, or content whose purpose is to harass, intimidate or
    stalk a particular person.
  * Content that is illegal to possess or distribute where you are.

This list is short on purpose. It is the conduct that gets somebody removed
rather than a description of everything anybody might dislike.

## How that is enforced, honestly

**We cannot read your messages.** Not as a policy and not as a promise we could
quietly break: the keys exist on the two phones and nowhere else, and the
mailbox that carries an envelope cannot open one. So this section describes
what actually happens rather than what a moderation page usually says.

**Blocking is yours and it is immediate.** Hold a member's name in a
conversation and block them. Nothing they send after that is written down,
counted, shown, or notified about on your device. They are not told, and they
stay in the conversation for everybody else. It needs nobody's permission and
no network.

**Reporting goes to the conversation.** Holding a message offers a report with
a reason. It reaches everybody in that conversation, including the person being
reported, and the screen says so before you send one. It does not reach us,
because a report we could act on would be a conversation we could read.
Whoever can admit and remove members is who can act on it, and removing
somebody is a change to the group that every member sees.

In a conversation of two there is nobody to report to who is not the person you
would be reporting, so the application offers blocking there instead. Blocking
is the thing that helps.

**What reaches us, and when we answer.** Anything sent to
<contact@ideoa.co.uk> is read by a person, and we answer within 24 hours. What
we can do depends on what you send us: we can remove a mailbox operator's
service from the application's defaults, act on a report about the
application itself, and pass material to the authorities where we are required
to. We cannot delete a message from somebody's phone, because we have never
had it.

## The mailbox

The default mailbox is run by Ideoa Labs. It holds sealed envelopes for up to
seven days on the free tier and cannot read one. You may point the application
at another one in Settings, in which case these terms cover the application
and whoever runs that mailbox covers the mailbox.

## What is not promised

Rotelyx is provided as it is. Messages can fail to arrive, a mailbox can be
unreachable, and a self destructing message depends on the other device
honouring the request, which a modified client need not do. The application
says so where it matters rather than only here.

## Changes

This document changes when the application does. The date at the top is when it
